Automate the boring handoffs first

Most teams look for automation inside a single tool. The bigger savings hide in the handoffs: from form to CRM, from sales to onboarding, from finished project to invoice.

Find the copy and paste

Ask your team where they copy data from one place to another. Each answer is a candidate for automation.

Keep a human in the loop

Not every handoff should be fully automatic. A quick approval step keeps quality high while still removing most of the manual work.
