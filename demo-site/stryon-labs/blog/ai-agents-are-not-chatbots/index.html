AI agents are not chatbots

When people hear "AI agent", they often picture a chat window. In our projects, the most useful agents never talk to customers at all. They work in the background: reading a new lead's website, filling in the CRM, flagging the deals that need attention.

Background work beats conversation

A background agent has a narrow job, clear inputs and an output someone can check. That makes it easier to trust, test and improve than an open-ended chat.

Start with one task

Pick a task your team does every day, with a clear "done". Automate that first, measure the hours saved, then move on to the next one.
